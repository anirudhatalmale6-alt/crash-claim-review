Our team reviews what you sent and may telephone you to ask a few more questions. If it looks like you may have a claim, your information is referred to a participating law firm. Whether that firm accepts your case is entirely their decision, and no one is obliged to take it.

Attorney fees

Motor vehicle accident cases are normally handled on a contingency basis, meaning the attorney is paid a percentage of any recovery and nothing if there is no recovery. Any firm that takes your case is required to explain its fee agreement to you in writing before you agree to it.

Services described here are not available in every state, and not every enquiry can be helped. Some cases cannot be accepted for reasons that have nothing to do with the strength of the claim.
