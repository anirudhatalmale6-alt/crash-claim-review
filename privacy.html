Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Read this before you submit the form. The details you enter are passed to a law firm or its agents so that someone can contact you about a possible claim. That is the whole purpose of this site.

What we collect

What you type into the form: your name, telephone number, email address, the state where the accident happened, and your answers to the four questions about the accident.

We also receive ordinary technical information automatically — your IP address, browser and device type, the page you arrived from, and which advertisement you clicked. We use a consent-certification service (TrustedForm) that records what this page looked like at the moment you submitted it, so there is a record of what you agreed to.

Why we collect it

To work out whether you may have a claim worth pursuing, and to put you in touch with a law firm that handles motor vehicle accident cases.

Who we share it with

With the law firm or representative to whom your enquiry is referred, and with the service providers who run our forms, spreadsheets and telephone systems. We do not sell your information to unrelated advertisers or list brokers.

Once your information has been passed to a firm, their own privacy policy governs what they do with it. We cannot withdraw it on your behalf after it has been referred.

How you will be contacted

By submitting the form you agree that we, the firm your enquiry is referred to, and their agents may contact you at the telephone number and email address you provided — including by automated dialling system, prerecorded message and text — about your potential claim. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or of obtaining legal representation. Message and data rates may apply.

You can stop this at any time. Reply STOP to any text, tell the caller you want to be removed, or write to the address below.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle the referral and meet our record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. Contact us using the details below and we will not treat you differently for asking.

Children

This service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
